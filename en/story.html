2011–2018

From the sports club to the classroom

I've loved working with children and young people ever since I was a teenager. It all started as a volunteer at TSV Lorch: first helping with children's gymnastics, then coaching competitive gymnastics. Before long, I knew I wanted to make that passion my career. From 2011 to 2016, I studied to become a teacher at the University of Education in Schwäbisch Gmünd. I chose Mathematics and Physics, along with Physical Education, inspired by my love of movement and science. After graduating, I completed my teacher training at Kastell Realschule in Welzheim, finishing in 2018.

2018–2021

Teaching, school IT & my first steps in web design

After my teacher training, I moved to Mainz to be with my boyfriend, who is now my husband. From 2018 to 2021, I taught students in years 5 to 10 at Realschule Plus in Gau-Odernheim. Alongside teaching, I quickly discovered how much I enjoyed the digital side of school life. As the school's digital learning coordinator, I managed our iServ platform, looked after mobile device management for teachers' and students' tablets, and helped set up the Apple TVs in our classrooms. I kept finding ways to be creative with technology outside work, too. My love of cooking led me to create my own food blog, where I got my first hands-on experience with web design.

2021

A new start in Sweden: moving into media computing

In 2021, my husband and I took a big leap and decided to leave Germany. Moving to Sweden began a new chapter in my life and a change in direction at work. I stepped away from teaching and enrolled in the part-time, distance-learning B.Sc. in Media Computing at IU International University of Applied Sciences. I wanted to build a solid foundation for my interests in IT, design and logical problem-solving.

2021–2025

Taking what I learned into an e-commerce project

The things I was learning didn't stay theory for long. Alongside my studies, I built a large and complex online shop for motorcycle graphics and accessories for my husband's company, Independent Racing. It gave me the chance to put what I'd learned into practice and gain valuable experience in web development and e-commerce. The shop and a few other website projects took more time than I'd expected, so my studies had to take a bit of a back seat for a while. Now that the shop launched successfully in 2025, I'm focused again on completing my degree and taking on more interesting IT projects.

2026

Looking ahead

Sweden is home now, and I've also found a way to bring my different interests together in my work. My background in education, hands-on e-commerce experience and media computing studies give me a strong foundation for creating digital products that are not only functional, but genuinely easy to use. Cooking and time in Swedish nature help me switch off and make room for new ideas. As I work towards finishing my degree, I'm looking forward to the next chapter: new projects, new connections and creative challenges in web design and development.
