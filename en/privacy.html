2. Processing of personal data

This website collects and processes personal data only to the extent necessary. This includes the following areas.

2.1 Contact form

If you contact me using the contact form, I store the information you provide, including your contact details (name, email address and message), so I can respond to your enquiry and follow up if needed. I will not share this data without your consent.

This data is processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or is necessary for pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, processing is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

2.2 Cookies & tracking

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools that collect personal data.

2.3 Server log files

The provider of this website automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser sends to us. This includes:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL
  • Hostname of the device accessing the website
  • Time of the server request
  • IP address (in anonymised form)

This data cannot be attributed to specific individuals. It is not combined with data from other sources. The data is used solely for statistical purposes and to improve the website. The legal basis for processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
